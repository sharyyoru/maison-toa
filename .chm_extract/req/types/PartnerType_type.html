PartnerType enumeration type
PartnerType is the type/flavor of a partner encoded by a terminal set of tokens. The partner specification was primarily demanded by the "FinMa - Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority" to enhance the billing transparency in VVG cases.
Set of enumeration values 16 elements
enEmployer: 1 the role/flavor of the partner is employer
enReferrer: 2 the role/flavor of the partner is referrer
enServiceProvider: 3 the role/flavor of the partner is service provider|service responsible that is an entity that does provide a service or is responsible for the provision of the service
enPrimaryClinician: 4 the role/flavor of the partner is primary clinician that is a synonym of the main treating physician
enLeadDoctor: 5 the role/flavor of the partner is lead doctor that is an entity that is responsible for the whole case
enAssistantPhysician: 6 the role/flavor of the partner is an assistant physician in the current treatment
enSeniorPhysician: 7 the role/flavor of the partner is a senior physician in the current treatment
enChiefPhysician: 8 the role/flavor of the partner is a chief physician in the current treatment
enSurgeon: 9 the role/flavor of the partner is a surgeon in the current treatment.
enAnaesthetist: 10 the role/flavor of the partner is an anaesthetist in the current treatment
enConsultantDoctor: 11 the role/flavor of the partner is a consultant doctor in the current treatment
enInternist: 12 the role/flavor of the partner is an internist in the current treatment
enCoCare: 13 the role/flavor of the partner is a co-carer in the current treatment
enRadiologist: 14 the role/flavor of the partner is radiologist in the current treatment
enNuclearMedicine: 15 the role/flavor of the partner is a nuclear medicine specialist in the current treatment
enOther: 16 the role/flavor of the partner is other that is none of the specific pre-defined roles holds true
